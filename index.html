How to use this lab

Start with a research question, then inspect the questionnaire and codebook. Check that the survey actually measures the election and constructs you want to study. Generate a plan, adapt the illustrative code to your verified variable names and codes, and document each decision.

Teaching resource. It does not download or analyse data, verify links, or provide causal identification.

From Research Questions to Empirical Analysis

Turn your research idea into a clear, practical plan for quantitative analysis.

Have a research question but aren't sure how to investigate it using data? This interactive toolkit guides you through the main steps of designing an empirical analysis, from identifying and measuring variables to choosing statistical methods and interpreting findings.

Describe what you want to explain, what you think might explain it, and where you plan to investigate the relationship. The toolkit will help you:

  • Translate theoretical concepts into measurable variables.
  • Explore data using descriptive statistics and visualisations.
  • Choose appropriate statistical methods and consider control variables.
  • Develop an analysis plan with example code for R and Stata.
  • Interpret findings and recognise the limitations of your analysis.

You don't need to have everything figured out before starting. This toolkit helps you think through methodological choices; it does not read your dataset, verify its variables or run analyses for you. Always consult the original questionnaire and codebook.

How to use this lab

Start with a research question, then inspect the questionnaire and codebook. Check that the survey actually measures the election and constructs you want to study. Generate a plan, adapt the illustrative code to your verified variable names and codes, and document each decision.

Teaching resource. It does not download or analyse data, verify links, or provide causal identification.
